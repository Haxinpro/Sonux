# Bundled audio test material

The test clips listed below are derived from works released under
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0). Attribution is not required, but the source
information is retained here for transparency.

The application copies short excerpts, resamples them to 48 kHz stereo PCM16,
normalizes their level, and adds short fades. The `.s16le` files contain raw,
headerless, little-endian PCM samples.
